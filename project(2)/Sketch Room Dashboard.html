Anatomy · Room card

Every card is a
miniature of the work.

Instead of generic thumbnails, each room shows a live snapshot of the canvas — the same moodboard, flowchart, or whiteboard your team is drawing in right now. Cards are pressure-tested for scanning.

01
Live indicator
A pulsing dot on rooms with people currently editing. Sits over the preview so it's visible at a glance.
02
Collaborator stack
Overlapping avatars use warm/cool hue pairing so identity stays clear even with 5+ people.
03
Contextual metric
Edits, comments, or version — whichever number is most relevant to that room type.
04
Kebab on hover
Owner-only actions (Rename, Duplicate, Delete) stay hidden until intent.
